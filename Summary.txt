1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
ClaimClarity is an independent civic-tech evidence reconciliation engine and
diagnostic support system for Indian citizens navigating contradictory,
fragmented, and delayed Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) claims.

Citizens frequently encounter confusing, out-of-order, or contradictory updates
across multiple independent touchpoints—such as the Member e-Sewa Unified Portal,
the EPFiGMS Grievance Portal, SMS status alerts, passbook transaction ledgers,
and bank account statements. ClaimClarity ingests multi-source evidence records,
extracts objective factual observations with runtime schema validation, partitions
multi-claim and multi-form contexts, performs multi-signal payment attribution,
evaluates timeline chronology and staleness, executes a purely deterministic
reconciliation state machine, maps diagnostic rejection reasons to actionable
guidance, renders a structured Verification Matrix, and generates an independent
Evidence & Grievance Support Dossier (PDF).

ClaimClarity is not an official government portal, claims tracker, calculator,
or free-form probabilistic chatbot. It operates as a privacy-first, stateless
evidence-organizing utility that refuses to guess when data is insufficient.


2. CORE PROBLEM SOLVED
Indian PF members withdrawing or transferring accumulated balances face three
systemic clarity hurdles:
  A. Out-of-Order & Asynchronous Record Latency: An older portal tracker may show
     "Under Process" days after a bank credit has already occurred, or an SMS may
     arrive out of sequence.
  B. Contradictory Information: Incompatible statuses (e.g. an official rejection
     notification juxtaposed with a bank credit entry) cause severe citizen anxiety,
     often triggering premature duplicate claims or costly grievance escalations.
  C. Technical Jargon & Unspecified Rejection Codes: Official field office rejection
     remarks (e.g. "Signature mismatch", "Member details differ", "Annexure K pending")
     leave citizens uninformed about what specific prerequisite action is required.

ClaimClarity resolves these hurdles by providing four simple, definitive answers:
  1. What is the actual evidence-backed status of my claim?
  2. Why did ClaimClarity reach this conclusion (with concrete proof)?
  3. What specific action should I take next?
  4. What mistakes should I avoid right now (e.g. "Don't file a duplicate claim")?


3. FINAL ARCHITECTURE
The production system is architected as a layered, unidirectional, fail-safe pipeline:

  User Evidence (Screenshots, Text, Portal extracts, Passbooks, SMS)
      │
      ▼
  Multimodal AI Extraction (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash via @google/genai)
  [Extracts ONLY structured factual observations; makes NO state decisions]
      │
      ▼
  Strict Schema Validation (Zod runtime parsing & error boundaries)
  [Rejects schema drift, malformed dates, invalid currencies]
      │
      ▼
  Claim & Form Partitioning Layer (lib/entity/formPartition.ts)
  [Separates records by Claim ID & Form type; prevents cross-claim contamination]
      │
      ▼
  Payment Attribution Engine (lib/reconciliation/attribution.ts)
  [Separates payment existence from payment ownership via multi-signal matching]
      │
      ▼
  Chronological & Staleness Analysis (lib/reconciliation/reconcileClaim.ts)
  [Sorts events, identifies superseded in-flight records, flags chronological regressions]
      │
      ▼
  Deterministic Reconciliation State Machine (lib/reconciliation/reconcileClaim.ts)
  [Pure rule-based state evaluator; emits exact status, confidence, and winning rationale]
      │
      ▼

      │

      │

      ▼                               ▼                               ▼
  Citizen Web Interface       Verification Matrix         Dossier Generator (PDF)
  (Next.js React UI)     (Structured audit breakdown) (Deterministic PDF document)


4. COMPLETE DATA FLOW
  Step 1 — Input Ingestion: The user inputs claim text, uploads screenshots (PNG,
          JPG, PDF), or selects a sample scenario.
  Step 2 — Structured Extraction: Backend `/api/analyze` sends raw artifacts to
          Gemini 2.5 Flash (or demo dispatcher) with structured JSON schemas.
  Step 3 — Zod Parsing: Extracted artifacts are parsed into strictly typed
          `Artifact` and `ClaimEvent` schemas.
  Step 4 — Partitioning: `partitionEvidence()` groups records by normalized claim
          reference numbers and form types (Form 19, 10C, 31, 13, 10D, 20).
  Step 5 — Payment Attribution: `attributePayments()` examines payment events against
          claim contexts, evaluating explicit references, EPFO sender labels, amount
          matches, and corroborating settlement milestones.
  Step 6 — Rejection Mapping: If a terminal rejection is detected, `classifyRejection()`
          matches regex patterns to one of 12 deterministic categories.
  Step 7 — State Evaluation: `reconcileClaim()` executes pure rule-based logic to
          determine the winning canonical status (`CREDITED`, `SETTLED`, `APPROVED`,
          `PROCESSING`, `SUBMITTED`, `REJECTED`, or `UNKNOWN`).
  Step 8 — Verification Matrix: `buildVerificationMatrix()` generates a granular
          breakdown of verified vs observed facts for auditability.
  Step 9 — Dossier Compilation: The user can trigger `/api/dossier` to download a
          formal, 11-section printable PDF evidence package.

8. PACKAGE 3 — REJECTION INTELLIGENCE & TIMING
  - Objective: Implement structured rejection diagnostics and centralized timing rules.
  - Implementations:
    * 12-category deterministic rejection taxonomy in `lib/reconciliation/rejections.ts`:
      1. `REJ_KYC_IDENTITY_MISMATCH` (Name, DoB, Father/Spouse name)
      2. `REJ_BANK_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` (Bank IFSC, invalid passbook/cheque)
      3. `REJ_SERVICE_ELIGIBILITY` (Service duration < 6 months, NCP days)
      4. `REJ_DUPLICATE_CLAIM` (Overlapping active or settled claims)
      5. `REJ_MEMBER_SIGNATURE_DOCS` (Unsigned Form 19/10C, uncertified docs)
      6. `REJ_CONTRIBUTION_WAGE_DISCREPANCY` (Wage ceiling mismatch, missing share)
      7. `REJ_ESTABLISHMENT_CLOSED_UNATTACHED` (Closed company, non-contributing)
      8. `REJ_FORM_PURPOSE_INELIGIBLE` (Form 31 para ineligibility, illness limits)
      9. `REJ_FATHER_SPOUSE_NAME_MISMATCH` (Discrepant parentage/marital records)
      10. `REJ_TRANSFER_ANNEXURE_K` (Annexure K pending from previous field office)
      11. `REJ_TECHNICAL_SYSTEM_ERROR` (Field office server/batch processing failure)
      12. `REJ_UNSPECIFIED` (Safe fallback when rejection text is ambiguous)
    * 4-Stage Resolution Ladder: `INFORM` -> `PREVENT_PROTECT` -> `REMEDIATE` -> `ESCALATE`.
    * Centralized Timing Model in `lib/config/timings.ts`:
      - 20-day Citizen Charter benchmark (informational guideline)
      - 30-day stale pending threshold
      - 45-day ClaimClarity escalation threshold
  - Status: COMPLETE & FROZEN.


9. PACKAGE 4 — PAYMENT ATTRIBUTION & MULTI-FORM / MULTI-ENTITY
  - Objective: Prevent false settlement conclusions and cross-claim contamination.
  - Implementations:
    * Form & Claim Partitioning in `lib/entity/formPartition.ts`:
      - Distinguishes distinct claim references (`CLM-1` vs `CLM-2`).
      - Distinguishes distinct form types (Form 19 vs 10C vs 31).
      - Prevents cross-claim contamination (Claim A settled does not mask Claim B rejected).
      - Isolates generic or unlinked records into `unresolvedArtifactIds`.
    * Payment Attribution in `lib/reconciliation/attribution.ts`:
      - Separates payment existence from payment ownership.
      - Classifies payments into: `ATTRIBUTED`, `CANDIDATE`, `UNATTRIBUTED`, `CONFLICTED`.
      - Requires explicit claim reference or settlement corroboration for `ATTRIBUTED`.
      - Prevents `UNATTRIBUTED`, `CANDIDATE`, and `CONFLICTED` from producing `CREDITED`.
  - Status: COMPLETE & FROZEN.

11. AI / EXTRACTION ARCHITECTURE
  - Component: `lib/ai/extractEvidence.ts`
  - Model: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (`@google/genai` SDK)
  - Strict Boundary: Multimodal vision & language parsing is used EXCLUSIVELY to
    extract text, dates, raw status strings, claim IDs, form names, and monetary
    amounts into strongly typed JSON structures.
  - Fail-Safe Guard: If `GEMINI_API_KEY` is not present, the system operates in
    hermetic Demo Mode, routing pre-extracted synthetic records directly into the
    deterministic reconciliation engine without external calls.
  - Non-Authority Rule: The AI model NEVER determines whether a claim is approved,
    settled, credited, or rejected.


12. DETERMINISTIC RECONCILIATION ARCHITECTURE
  - Component: `lib/reconciliation/reconcileClaim.ts`
  - Paradigm: Pure mathematical and rule-based state machine.
  - Canonical Status Hierarchy:
    `CREDITED` > `SETTLED` > `APPROVED` > `PROCESSING` > `SUBMITTED` > `REJECTED` > `UNKNOWN`
  - Core Decision Principles:
    * Chronology & Supersession: A later terminal outcome (Settled/Credited) supersedes
      earlier in-flight processing records.
    * Terminal Contradiction: Mutually exclusive terminal states (Rejection vs Credit
      for the same claim context) yield `UNKNOWN` state with `TERMINAL_CONTRADICTION` warning.
    * Evidence Sufficiency Floor: Vague, undated, or unidentifiable records refuse to guess
      and yield `UNKNOWN` with actionable guidance on missing facts.
    * Mathematical Confidence: High (corroborated & uncontradicted), Medium (supported
      with minor latency/in-flight ambiguity), Low (insufficient or conflicted).


13. CLAIM / FORM PARTITIONING
  - Component: `lib/entity/formPartition.ts`
  - Invariants:
    * Same member != Same claim (Multiple concurrent claims remain separate).
    * Form type != Claim identity (Form 19 and Form 10C are distinct claims).
    * Unresolved evidence remains in `unresolvedArtifactIds` rather than being falsely
      assigned to the first available claim.
    * Discordant identifiers generate `IDENTIFIER_MISMATCH` conflicts.


14. PAYMENT ATTRIBUTION
  - Component: `lib/reconciliation/attribution.ts`
  - Invariants:
    * Bank credit existence != Claim attribution.
    * Amount match alone is NOT sufficient for attribution (remains `CANDIDATE`).
    * Timing proximity alone is NOT sufficient for attribution.
    * Sender label alone (`EPFO-NEFT`) is NOT claim ownership.
    * `ATTRIBUTED` requires direct claim reference matching OR single context with
      explicit EPFO sender AND corroborating settlement milestone evidence.
    * Only `ATTRIBUTED` payments can transition a claim to `CREDITED`.


15. REJECTION INTELLIGENCE
  - Component: `lib/reconciliation/rejections.ts`
  - Engine: Deterministic keyword & regex matcher mapping raw rejection snippets to
    the 12-category taxonomy.
  - Output: Diagnostic title, plain-language interpretation, resolution prerequisites,
    concrete corrective action, "Don't Do This Yet" warning, and escalation rules.
  - Fallback: Ambiguous phrases safely degrade to `REJ_UNSPECIFIED`.


16. TIMING MODEL
  - Component: `lib/config/timings.ts`
  - Rules:
    * Benchmark: 20 days (EPFO Citizen Charter informational guideline).
    * Stale Pending: 30 days without milestone updates.
    * Escalation Threshold: 45 days elapsed from submission date.
  - Representation: Presented strictly as service benchmarks, never as official legal rulings.

28. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - Lack of Live EPFO Server Access: Operates exclusively on user-supplied records; cannot query live government databases.
  - Ambiguous Bank Narrations: Bank credits with truncated or missing claim IDs remain as `CANDIDATE` or `UNATTRIBUTED`.
  - PDF Font Character Metrics: The lightweight pure TypeScript PDF engine maps non-Latin Unicode characters (such as Devnagari glyphs) to clean Roman transliterations and standard Type-1 font metrics to guarantee 100% crash-free, zero-dependency PDF generation. Full native Devnagari text is supported throughout the web UI.


29. SECURITY / TRUST BOUNDARIES
  - No Secret Leakage: API keys and environment variables (`GEMINI_API_KEY`) remain strictly backend-only.
  - Zero Third-Party Uploads: Evidence and PDFs are processed strictly within the local application runtime without third-party document APIs.
  - Input Validation: Strict Zod schemas guard against payload tampering and injection attacks.

31. IMPORTANT DESIGN INVARIANTS
  1. AI extracts facts; deterministic code makes final decisions.
  2. Same member != Same claim context.
  3. Form type != Claim identity.
  4. Unresolved evidence stays unresolved.
  5. Conflicting identifiers stay conflicted.
  6. Bank payment exists != Bank payment belongs to this claim.
  7. Amount match alone != Payment attribution.
  8. Timing proximity alone != Payment attribution.
  9. Sender label alone != Claim ownership.
  10. Unattributed payment != CREDITED state.
  11. Candidate payment != Automatic CREDITED state.
  12. Conflicted payment != CREDITED state.
  13. Historical rejection from one claim must never contaminate another claim.
  14. 20-day Citizen Charter benchmark is informational, not a legal violation.
  15. ClaimClarity diagnostic category is an evidence assessment, not an official EPFO rejection code.
  16. ClaimClarity Evidence Dossier is an independent aid, not an official EPFO or EPFiGMS submission.
  17. Frontend must not expose Gemini/model/provider/prototype/MVP implementation details.
  18. The system is stateless and stores zero citizen data.
